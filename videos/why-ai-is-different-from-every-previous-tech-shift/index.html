Why AI is Different From Every Previous Tech Shift

Every previous technological shift made humans faster, more efficient, or better connected. AI is fundamentally different because it is the first technology that transitions from being a tool to becoming a creator.

About this video

In this talk, Kanth explores how AI has evolved from early algorithms and computing hardware to general-purpose transformers. He breaks down the structural shift in software economics: as the cost of building software drops toward zero, the value moves away from execution and toward distribution, trust, and human connection.

Key takeaways

  • Why AI is a fundamental shift rather than an incremental upgrade
  • How hardware, data, and transformers converged to create modern AI
  • The shifting value chain: why distribution matters more than execution
  • The role of human elements like trust, empathy, and collaboration in an AI-driven world
